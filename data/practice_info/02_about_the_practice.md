# About us

The practice has provided dental care in Warrington since 1929, and has
offered NHS dental services for more than fifty years.

Alongside NHS care, the practice offers private and independent treatment.
That ranges from tooth coloured fillings and gum treatment through to
dentures and dental implants.

# The team

The practice is led by a principal dentist who is also a practice partner,
with a second partner working as an associate dentist. There is a dentist
with a special interest in root canal treatment who sees private patients
for endodontic consultations and treatment.

Alongside the dentists there are dental hygiene therapists, dental nurses,
a management and senior nursing team, and reception and administrative
staff.

Student dentists sometimes treat patients under the supervision of the
principal dentist.

# Regulation

The practice is registered with the Care Quality Commission. The registered
provider is ICS Dental Limited.

# Accessibility and parking

There is a portable ramp available for wheelchair and pushchair access.
There is car parking nearby, though not on site.
