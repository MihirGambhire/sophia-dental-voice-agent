# Booking an NHS appointment

NHS appointments cannot be booked through the website or by email. They
have to be booked over the phone.

# Booking a private appointment

Private examinations and private hygienist appointments can be booked
online at any time of day through the practice booking system, or over the
phone.

A deposit is taken when an examination is booked online. The full fee is
taken when a hygiene appointment is booked. Those payments are not
refunded if the patient does not attend, or cancels with less than 24
hours notice.

# Joining as a new NHS patient

NHS capacity at the practice is limited. New NHS patients are accepted only
where capacity allows.

The online waiting list is currently paused because demand is very high.

The NHS website has also listed the practice as accepting new NHS patients
for routine care when availability allows, covering adults, adults entitled
to free care, and children aged 17 and under. Availability changes, so the
honest answer to give a caller is that capacity is limited right now, the
waiting list is paused, and they are welcome to leave their details for the
team to call back. Patients can also check the current position on the NHS
website.

# Joining as a private patient

The practice does accept new private patients. Ring the practice to book.
The first private examination for a new patient costs £85 and is payable at
the time of booking.

# Staying registered

Patients need to attend routine appointments to stay registered with the
practice. If someone has not attended for more than three years, they are no
longer registered, and would be treated as a new patient when they come
back.

# Appointment reminders

The practice no longer sends email or text reminders about booking routine
appointments. Remembering to book a check up is the patient's own
responsibility.

# Demand

The practice receives enquiries from people wanting to register all the
time, but the number of places it can offer is limited.
