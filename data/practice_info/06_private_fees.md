# Examinations

- New patient examination or consultation, including radiographs if
  required: £85. This applies to patients who are new to the practice, or
  who have not been seen for at least three years. It is payable when the
  appointment is booked.
- Routine examination, including radiographs if required: £50. For patients
  who are seen regularly and have already had a new patient consultation.
- Review appointment with a dentist or dental therapist, for example a gum
  review, with no radiographs: £40.

# Emergency treatment

- Emergency consultation for a new patient, meaning someone who has never
  visited or has not had a routine or new patient examination in three
  years: £85.
- Emergency consultation for an existing patient, seen for routine
  treatment within the last three years: £50.
- Routine extraction: £145, charged in addition to the assessment fee.
- Surgical extraction: from £325, charged in addition to the assessment fee.
- Emergency dressing for a painful socket after an extraction: £40.
- Recementing a crown or bridge is on the price list, and the team will
  confirm the fee.

# Hygiene and gum treatment

These appointments are with a dental hygiene therapist and are priced by
length.

- Up to 20 minutes: £55
- Up to 30 minutes: £75
- Up to 40 minutes: £95
- Up to 60 minutes: £135, for extensive gum disease or heavy staining

Hygienist direct access costs £140. That is for seeing a hygiene therapist
without seeing a dentist first, and it applies to patients who have not
been seen at the practice before, or who have not been seen for at least
three years.

NHS hygiene treatment is only available after a dentist has assessed the
patient and referred them.

# Root canal treatment with the special interest dentist

- Consultation: £85
- Incisor or canine: from £450
- Premolar: from £470
- Molar: from £595

# Root canal treatment with a general dentist

- Incisor or canine: £320. This does not include the fee for the filling or
  crown that restores the tooth afterwards.

# Fees not listed here

The practice also charges for fillings, imaging, implants, tooth whitening,
crowns and bridges, orthodontics, mouth guards and bite guards, and
dentures. Those prices are not held in this demo. For anything not listed,
the right answer is that the team will confirm a quote after an
assessment, and to offer to take a message.
