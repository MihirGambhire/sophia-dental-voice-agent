# Opening hours

The practice is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.

It closes for lunch between 1pm and 2pm every weekday. No appointments are
booked during that hour.

The practice is closed on Saturdays and Sundays, and on bank holidays.

# Where we are

Museum Street Dental Practice, 11-13 Museum Street, Warrington, Cheshire,
WA1 1JA. It sits just off Warrington town centre.

# Phone

The practice number is 01925 630221. The phones are answered during
opening hours, 8am to 6pm on weekdays.

# Out of hours

If you need urgent dental help when the practice is closed:

- Patients in Liverpool, Wirral and Cheshire can call the out of hours
  emergency dental service on 0161 476 9651.
- Anyone can call NHS 111 for urgent care advice, including people who are
  not registered with a dentist.
- For a life threatening emergency, call 999.

# Note on an inconsistency

The practice website gives two different times for ringing about urgent
treatment. One page says to call from 8am, another mentions 9am to 5pm.
This demo uses 8am as the release time for same day urgent appointments,
and the inconsistency is recorded in the project README.
